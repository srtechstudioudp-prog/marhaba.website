About Marhaba Heavy Equipment

Marhaba Heavy Equipment Trading LLC is a Sharjah-based trading company that supplies lifting, earth-moving, mining and construction equipment and spare parts. Our small team handles each enquiry from the first offer to shipment, and we work with sources in Japan, the USA and India.

  • Three product lines: cranes, heavy equipment and spare parts
  • Global sourcing: network that includes Japan, the USA and India
  • Evidence first: inspection reports and pictures before you order

A buyer in East Africa needs a replacement hydraulic pump for an excavator that has been parked for two weeks. Every day idle costs the contractor more than the pump. That is the type of urgency that shaped how we work: quick answers, clear photos, and honest information about what is and is not available.

Who we are

Marhaba Heavy Equipment Trading LLC aims to meet the rising demand for lifting, earth-moving, mining and construction equipment. We are a trading company, which means we connect buyers to machines and parts rather than manufacturing them.

Our team handles client enquiries and keeps clients updated from the moment an order is placed until the shipment leaves. We believe every customer is special, every inquiry is precious and every order is valuable.

What we supply

We focus on three groups of products. Each has its own page with practical buying information.

  • Cranes: all terrain, truck mounted, crawler and tower cranes
  • Heavy equipment: excavators, dozers, wheel loaders, graders, dump trucks, pipelayers, backhoe loaders and drilling machines
  • Spare parts: engine, hydraulic, transmission and undercarriage parts, mining and locomotive spares, EOT crane components

How an order works

The process has four steps and you stay in control of the decision.

  1. Inquiry: you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp with the machine or part you need.
  2. Offer: we send competitive offers for stock on hand or equipment in other locations worldwide.
  3. Order: you order after reviewing inspection reports and pictures.
  4. Shipment: we arrange sea or air freight to your destination port.

Frequently asked questions

Is Marhaba Heavy Equipment a manufacturer?

No. We are a trading company. We source and supply cranes, heavy equipment and spare parts, and we arrange shipment. We do not manufacture the machines.

Where are you based?

Office D20, Al Nayli Building, Bin Laden Street, Industrial Area 13, Sharjah, UAE. You can reach us by phone, WhatsApp or email, and our working hours are Sunday to Thursday, 8:00 to 18:00.

What kinds of equipment do you handle?

Cranes of several types, earth-moving and mining machines, and spare parts for engines, hydraulics, transmissions and undercarriages. The products page lists them all.

Which countries do you work with?

We work with sources in Japan, the USA and India and supply buyers in the UAE, Gulf countries, India, Africa, Asia and Europe. Ask us about any other destination.

Do you offer inspection reports?

Our process is that the customer orders after reviewing inspection reports and pictures. Tell us which checks matter to you, and we will request them for the machine in question.

Who will I talk to after I place an order?

A dedicated team member keeps you updated from order placement through shipment, so you do not have to chase different departments.

Can you supply a complete project package?

Yes, we can quote several machines and parts together. Send the full list and your delivery port, and we will respond with a combined offer.

How do I pay and ship?

Payment terms and freight are agreed in the offer for each order. We arrange sea or air freight to your destination port once terms are confirmed.

Do you publish prices?

No. Pricing depends on the model, condition and freight, so we quote each requirement individually.

How can I check the company details?

The legal name is Marhaba Heavy Equipment Trading LLC and the address is Office D20, Al Nayli Building, Bin Laden Street, Industrial Area 13, Sharjah. For trade licence details, ask us and we will share the documents you need for your due diligence.
