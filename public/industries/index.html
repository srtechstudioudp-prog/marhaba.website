Equipment for construction, mining, oil and gas

Marhaba Heavy Equipment supplies cranes, earth-moving machines and spare parts to the construction, mining, oil and gas, and infrastructure sectors. Each industry needs a different mix of lifting, digging, hauling and maintenance parts, and we help match the equipment to the job.

Two projects can ask for the same machine class and still need different things. The site contractor wants speed and availability, the mine wants wear life and parts support. Knowing the industry changes the questions we ask first.

What do construction contractors ask for?

Contractors usually need machines that are available soon and can be resold or moved between sites. Excavators, loaders and mobile cranes are the most common requests, with parts supplied to keep them working.

What do mining and quarry operators ask for?

Mining customers care about wear life, component availability and downtime. They ask for rigid dump trucks, loaders and drilling machines, plus mining spares and engine and transmission parts.

What about oil and gas?

Lifting is central. Crawler and all terrain cranes handle plant and pipeline work, and pipelayers support trench work. Spares for hydraulics and engines keep equipment on schedule.

What about roads and infrastructure?

Roads and rail projects need motor graders, dozers, excavators and heavy cranes for bridges. Rail operations also ask for locomotive spares.

What does each sector ask first?

Construction

Availability and timing come first. A contractor wants a machine that can start work soon and can be resold or moved to the next site. Size class, attachments and year range matter more than brand.

Mining and quarrying

Wear life, parts supply and downtime lead the conversation. Buyers ask about undercarriage and tyre condition, engine hours and whether a first set of wear parts can ship with the machine.

Oil and gas

Lifting capacity at the working radius, documentation and inspection records come first. Plant sites also need machines that fit tight access and strict safety rules.

Infrastructure

Roads, rail and bridges need a mix of graders, dozers, excavators and heavy cranes working to a programme. Buyers often request a package of machines and parts in one offer.
