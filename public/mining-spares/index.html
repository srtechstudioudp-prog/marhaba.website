How do I find the right part number?

  1. Read the part number on the old part or its box.
  2. Check the machine model and serial number plate.
  3. Type the number in the filter above.
  4. If it is not listed, send the number anyway. We can check other sources.

What should I send for a spare parts quote?

  • Part number and photo of the part
  • Machine brand, model and serial number
  • Quantity and urgency
  • Delivery country and port

Why match by part number, not by description?

Many parts look alike but differ in size, thread, pressure rating or material. Two gaskets can share a shape and fail on a different engine. The part number removes that guesswork, so always quote it with the machine serial number.
