A buyer in West Africa has found a suitable excavator but is unsure about port charges, loading and documents. The machine is the easy part. The risk is in the steps around it, so it helps to agree them in writing before payment.

How does the export process work?

  1. Inquiry: send the machine or part you need, with the delivery port.
  2. Offer: we send competitive offers for stock on hand or equipment in other locations worldwide.
  3. Order: you decide after reviewing inspection reports and pictures.
  4. Shipment: we arrange sea or air freight to your destination port and update you.

Which countries do we supply?

Our listings mention the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Qatar, India, Africa, Asia and Europe. If your port is elsewhere, send it and we will check the route.

What documents are usually involved?

Import rules differ widely by country. Confirm local requirements on used equipment age, emissions and certification before you order.

  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bill of lading or airway bill
  • Certificate of origin where required
  • Inspection reports and machine data
  • Import documents in your country: check local rules for age limits on used equipment

How do I prepare an enquiry for export?

  • Machine or part details and quantity
  • Destination port and country
  • Required year range and condition
  • Whether you need spare parts shipped with the machine
  • Your local regulatory requirements

What does the machine need before shipping?

Typically it is cleaned, checked and its fluids and attachments are secured, with pictures taken for records. Details depend on the machine and carrier, and are agreed in the offer.

Shipping terms explained

Who pays for what

Trade terms such as FOB or CIF set who pays for loading, freight and insurance, and where risk passes to the buyer. Agree the term in writing and check what it covers before you accept an offer.

Insurance

Cargo insurance protects against damage or loss in transit. For high-value machines it is usually worth including, and your offer should say whether it is part of the price.

Loading and securing

Machines are lashed to the deck or inside a container, with booms stowed and fluids secured. Ask for loading photos so you can compare them with the machine on arrival.

What can go wrong, and how do we avoid it?

  • Wrong part ordered: confirm part and serial numbers first
  • Machine exceeds port or road limits: share dimensions early
  • Documents mismatch: check names and numbers before shipment
  • Unclear charges: agree who pays port and handling fees in writing

Frequently asked questions

Do you export from the UAE to Africa?

Yes. Our listings mention African markets. Send your destination port and we will quote the route and freight.

Can you export to India and Asia?

Yes. We have sourcing links in India and Japan and supply buyers in India and across Asia.

How is heavy equipment shipped?

Mostly by sea, either roll-on/roll-off, break bulk or container depending on size. Small or urgent parts can go by air.

Who arranges the freight?

We arrange sea or air freight to your destination port. Terms and charges are stated in the offer.

What documents will I receive?

Typically the invoice, packing list and transport document, plus the inspection reports and pictures. Ask for any additional document you need for customs.

Can I buy a machine and parts in one shipment?

Yes. Combining them can reduce freight cost. List both in your enquiry.

Are there age limits for used equipment?

Some countries restrict the age of imported used equipment. Check your local rules and tell us the maximum year or age you can import.

How do I track my shipment?

Your contact on our team keeps you updated from order placement to shipment, and you receive the transport documents that include the tracking references.

Do you handle customs clearance at my port?

Clearance at the destination is normally handled by your agent. Tell us if you need help coordinating and we will discuss options.
