What does Marhaba Heavy Equipment do?

We are a trading company in Industrial Area 13, Sharjah. Our work is matching a buyer with the right crane, earth-moving machine or spare part, then arranging the paperwork and freight to get it to site.

Our listings cover lifting equipment (all terrain, truck mounted, crawler and tower cranes), earth-moving and mining machines (excavators, dozers, wheel loaders, graders, dump trucks, pipelayers and drilling machines) and spare parts for engines, hydraulics, transmissions and undercarriages.

Why buyers send us their enquiries

Heavy equipment is expensive, and a wrong purchase costs months. Our approach is built around reducing that risk before an order is placed.

  • Offers from a wide network. We quote stock on hand and machines at other locations, with sourcing links in Japan, the USA and India.
  • Evidence before you commit. Inspection reports and pictures are reviewed before you place an order.
  • One contact from enquiry to shipment. A small team updates you from order placement through delivery.
  • Freight handled for you. Sea or air shipment to your destination port is arranged by us.
  • Parts and machines together. Ask for the machine and the wear parts it will need in one enquiry.

Industries we support

Our customers work in construction, mining, oil and gas, and infrastructure. A road contractor in the Gulf, a quarry in Africa and a plant in India need different machines, but they all ask the same first questions: what is available, what is the condition, and how soon can it ship.

Who is this for?

Contractors and fleet owners adding capacity, traders and exporters filling a customer order, maintenance managers who need a specific component, and project buyers sourcing a full equipment package. If you already know the model, send it. If you only know the job, describe the job and we will suggest what to look at.

Frequently asked questions

What does Marhaba Heavy Equipment Trading LLC sell?

We supply cranes (all terrain, truck mounted, crawler and tower), heavy equipment such as excavators, dozers, wheel loaders, motor graders, rigid dump trucks, pipelayers, backhoe loaders and drilling machines, and spare parts and components. See products for the full list.

Where is the company located?

We are based at Office D20, Al Nayli Building, Bin Laden Street, Industrial Area 13, Sharjah, United Arab Emirates. Our team is available Sunday to Thursday, 8:00 to 18:00. The contact page has our phone numbers and email.

Do you ship outside the UAE?

Yes. We arrange sea or air freight to the buyer's destination port. Our listings mention customers in the Gulf, India, Africa, Asia and Europe. See export from the UAE for how shipments are organised.

How do I request a quote?

Send the equipment type, brand, model or part number, quantity and delivery port by email, phone or WhatsApp, or use the form on the contact page. We reply with an offer for stock on hand or machines at other locations.

Can I see inspection reports and pictures before ordering?

Yes. Our process is that you order after reviewing inspection reports and pictures of the machine. Tell us what you want checked, such as working hours, hydraulics or undercarriage wear, and we will request it.

Do you sell spare parts as well as machines?

Yes. We handle engine, hydraulic, transmission and undercarriage parts, and mining spares, locomotive spares and EOT crane components. See spare parts.

What information do you need to quote a part?

The machine brand and model, serial number, part number from the old part, and a photo of the part or its plate. With those, we can match the right component and avoid ordering the wrong one.

Which brands can you source?

Customers most often ask for Caterpillar, Komatsu, Hitachi, Kobelco, Liebherr, Tadano, Kato and similar makes. Brand names are used for reference, and availability depends on the machine and location.

How long does delivery take?

It depends on whether the machine is in stock, where it is located, the freight mode and the destination port. We confirm the schedule in the offer instead of promising a fixed time up front.

Are prices listed on the website?

No. Prices depend on model, condition, year and freight, so each requirement gets its own offer. Send an enquiry and we will respond with a quotation.

Can you help me choose the right machine?

Yes. Describe the job, ground conditions, load or output you need and the delivery port. We will suggest suitable machine classes. Our guides also explain how to choose and inspect equipment.
