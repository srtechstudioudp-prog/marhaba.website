Which locomotives do these parts fit?

Fit depends on the locomotive model and the exact component. The client record for the fuel pump motor mentions suitability for EMD 567, 645 and 710 engines and ALCO GE diesel models. Always confirm the fit with the part number, the locomotive model and, where possible, a photo of the nameplate before you order.

How do I order a locomotive part?

  1. Find the part number on the old part or in your maintenance manual.
  2. Open the product page and check the listed rating or notes.
  3. Send the part number, locomotive model and quantity.
  4. Confirm the offer, delivery port and shipping method.

What should I send for a quote?

  • Part number and photo of the part or nameplate
  • Locomotive make, model and number
  • Voltage, current or other rating if known
  • Quantity and delivery port
