What to send us

The more detail you give, the faster and more accurate our offer.

  • Equipment type, brand and model (or the part number and machine serial number)
  • Quantity and preferred condition or year range
  • Working hours limit, capacity or output you need
  • Delivery country and destination port
  • Photos of the part, nameplate or work site if you have them
  • Your timeline and any documents you need, such as inspection reports

Frequently asked questions

What are your working hours?

We work Sunday to Thursday, 8:00 to 18:00 UAE time. You can send an enquiry on WhatsApp or email at any time and we reply during working hours.

What is the fastest way to get a quote?

Send the brand, model or part number, quantity and delivery port on WhatsApp or through the form. A complete enquiry lets us quote without back-and-forth.

Can I visit your office?

Please call before visiting so we can confirm the address and have the right person available. We are at Office D20, Al Nayli Building, Bin Laden Street, Industrial Area 13, Sharjah.

Do you reply in Arabic or Hindi?

Please write in English for the fastest handling. If you prefer another language, mention it in your message and we will try to arrange it.

I only have a photo of the part. Is that enough?

A clear photo plus the machine model and serial number is often enough for us to start matching. Add any numbers stamped on the part.

Do you quote by email or WhatsApp?

Both. Choose the channel you prefer in your enquiry and we will respond there, with documents attached where needed.

Can I request several machines in one enquiry?

Yes. List each machine or part on a separate line with quantity, and we will prepare one combined offer.

Is there a minimum order?

We have not set a published minimum. Tell us your quantity and we will say what is possible.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

We review it, check availability, and send an offer. After you review the offer and inspection details, you can place an order and we arrange shipment.

Where can I see which equipment you cover?

Start with cranes, heavy equipment and spare parts. Each has pages for the specific machine types.
