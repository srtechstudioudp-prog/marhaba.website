What affects the price of a used machine?

Age and hours

Year and working hours set the baseline, but wear depends on how and where the machine worked.

Condition of major parts

Engine, hydraulics and undercarriage or tyres are the largest cost items. Their condition moves the price more than cosmetics.

Location and freight

A machine in another country adds inland and sea freight. Compare the delivered cost, not only the machine price.

What documents should come with a used machine?

  • Serial number plate photo and matching papers
  • Service and maintenance records if available
  • Load chart and manuals for cranes
  • Inspection report and pictures
  • Export documents and invoice

Common mistakes when buying used

  • Buying from the hour meter alone
  • Not checking import age limits in your country
  • Ignoring tyre or undercarriage wear
  • Skipping the serial number match
  • Not asking what is included (attachments, counterweights, spare keys)

What to send for a used equipment quote

  • Attachments and accessories needed
  • Delivery port and timeline
  • Any import rules you must meet
