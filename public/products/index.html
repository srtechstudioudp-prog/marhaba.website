Which industries use this equipment?

Construction, mining, oil and gas, and infrastructure are the main users. See industries we support for what each sector typically needs.

What decisions come before an enquiry?

Decide the job first

Write down the lift weight, the digging depth or the output per day. Machine choice follows the job, and a clear job description shortens every later conversation.

Decide your condition and budget limits

State the year range and hour limit you will accept, and the budget range you are working to. We can then tell you early if the combination is realistic.

Decide how it reaches you

Name the destination port, who clears customs, and whether road transport at the other end can carry the machine. Freight and local rules change which machine makes sense.

Frequently asked questions

What types of cranes do you supply?

We supply all terrain, truck mounted, crawler and tower cranes. See the cranes page for how they differ and how to choose.

Which heavy equipment can I request?

Excavators, dozers, wheel loaders, motor graders, rigid dump trucks, pipelayers, backhoe loaders and drilling machines. Browse heavy equipment.

What spare parts do you handle?

Engine, hydraulic, transmission and undercarriage parts, mining spares, locomotive spares and EOT crane components. See spare parts.

Can I order a machine and its parts together?

Yes. List both in the same enquiry and we will quote them together, which can simplify freight.

Are prices shown on the site?

No. Prices depend on model, condition, year and freight. We send a written offer for each enquiry.

Which brands do you work with?

Customers commonly ask for Caterpillar, Komatsu, Hitachi, Kobelco, Liebherr, Tadano and Kato. Ask about others. Brand names are used for reference only.

How do I know a machine is in good condition?

You review inspection reports and pictures before ordering. Tell us the checks that matter to you, such as hours, hydraulics and undercarriage wear.

Do you ship outside the UAE?

Yes. We arrange sea or air freight to your destination port. See export.

How long does it take to get an offer?

We reply during working hours, Sunday to Thursday. Complete details such as model and delivery port let us respond faster.

What if I do not know the exact model?

Describe the job, load, ground and output you need. We will suggest machine classes and models to compare.
