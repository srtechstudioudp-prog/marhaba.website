Why match parts to the engine serial number?

The same engine model can have several specifications for power rating, emissions or application. A part that fits the model may still not suit your serial number. The engine data plate and the machine serial number let us confirm the correct part.

Benefits of regular engine parts replacement

  • Lower fuel use and cleaner running
  • Longer engine life with clean oil and air
  • Fewer breakdowns on site
  • Predictable overhaul costs

Applications

  • Excavators, loaders and dozers: all diesel-driven
  • Dump trucks and haul trucks: long daily hours
  • Generators and pumps: continuous or standby duty
  • Cranes: carrier and crane engines
  • Truck fleets: road trucks and service vehicles

How do I identify an engine part?

  1. Photograph the engine data plate and the old part.
  2. Note the machine brand, model and serial number.
  3. Write down the part number stamped on the old part.
  4. Describe the problem and symptoms.
  5. Send everything with your enquiry.

Common mistakes

  • Ordering by model only, without the serial number
  • Fitting a new injection pump without cleaning the fuel system
  • Using the wrong filter grade
  • Skipping oil and coolant analysis before an overhaul

Care and standards

Follow the engine manufacturer's service intervals for oil, filters and coolant. Emissions and fuel requirements differ by country. These are general notes, not claims about specific products.
